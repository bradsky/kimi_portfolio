About project

Binance Academy Course

Academy Course is an online educational program launched by Binance. Binance is committed to promoting knowledge related to the cryptocurrency industry and enhancing its image as a responsible social enterprise. Initially, it has partnered with schools in the United States to introduce courses suitable for students from K-12 to college, aiming to popularize knowledge related to cryptocurrency.

Goal

​​Establish a comprehensive Binance Course online service, collaborate with American schools, and integrate it into the curriculum of these institutions.

Role

Sr. UI/UX Designer

Challenge

🍄 Integrated Gamification Elements (e.g., NFT rewards) to increase active engagement and course retention.
🍄 Streamlined User Journey by consolidating video learning and assessment into a single view, minimizing navigation friction.

Learning from benchmark in online course industry

In the early stages, to better understand the design of online courses, I hosted a simple workshop with stakeholder to discuss the future functions and details of the service with relevant stakeholders.​​​​​​​

Since different users utilize the page builder in diverse ways, understanding the distinct needs and pain points requires a varied approach to interviews. Users who frequently engage in creation and editing actions often have more direct suggestions and feedback regarding the usage process. Consequently, I invited several such users for one-on-one interviews focused on the creation process and documented their insights.

Design Solution

After couple rounds UX phase discussion, there are some strategies we rolled out: 

  1. Simplify the information architecture of Binance Course for better cognitive load and course active rate. 

  2. Some trigger for increasing the course active rate. E.g., reward info, participation rate of course...etc.

  3. A NFT diploma or reward for those student who completes the course to increase the active rate.

In the landing level, user can see the track list and the related info of each track. We also listed out the course progress and reward in each track for better progress awareness.
